EDGAR Dashboard in Six Themes

Three pages of a Power BI report built on SEC EDGAR XBRL filings for ten US restaurant companies, rendered in each of the six themes the project's theme lab produces. Every figure on every screenshot is the same data — only the palette changes.

What you are looking at

Real screenshots from Power BI Desktop, cropped to the report canvas. The layout, the fields and the numbers are identical across all six; the theme supplies colour, corner radius and callout size, and nothing else.

Why the charts stop at 2024

2025 is loaded and carries 8,184 facts, but none of annual duration yet, so it has no column in any year view. Blank years are dropped rather than shown as zero.

Contrast

The ratio on each theme is its primary text against its panel colour, measured rather than assumed. All six clear WCAG AA for body text by a wide margin.
